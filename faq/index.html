Frequently Asked Questions

Everything guests commonly ask before booking a horseback safari with Namibia Horse Safaris — experience level, inclusions, timing, routes, and how to book.

What is the best time for a horse safari in Namibia?

The best time is April to October, the dry season, when temperatures are mild, skies are clear, and wildlife gathers around waterholes for more frequent sightings.

Do I need riding experience?

No. We welcome riders of all levels. Guides match each guest to a suitable horse and provide instruction — beginners get gentler horses and shorter safaris, while experienced riders can tackle longer, more challenging routes.

What's included in the packages?

Accommodation, meals, drinks, professional guides, well-trained horses, safety equipment, and park fees. Luxury camping and lodge-based options are available depending on the package.

What horse breeds do you ride?

A mix of Appaloosa, Arabian, Boerperd, Namib desert horses, Paint horses, and Quarter horses — chosen for stamina and suitability to desert terrain.

What safari packages are available?

Short Escape (5 days, 2 destinations), Tri-Land Safari (8 days, 3 destinations), Desert Explorer (7 days), Great Five Safari (12 days, 5 destinations), Ultimate Namibia (16 days, all hotspots), and a Volunteer & Train program (3 weeks, lodge based).

Where do the safaris take place?

Across the Namib Desert, the Skeleton Coast, Etosha National Park, Sossusvlei, and the Swakopmund area — combining dunes, riverbeds, mountain passes and coastal plains.

What accommodation is provided?

Luxury camping or lodge-based stays depending on the route and package, arranged as part of the safari price.
